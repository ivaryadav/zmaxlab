# Media Asset Review
**Date:** 2026-08-08
**Source folder:** "ZmaxLab Website images and videos" (30 JPGs, 10 MP4s, ~66 MB)
**Origin:** Pexels stock library — **not** real ZmaxLab client photography.

## Important framing
This library closes the "no photography" gap flagged in `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`. It does **not** close the "no real client proof" gap flagged in Phases 1, 2, and 9 — those are different problems. Stock clinicians must never be captioned or positioned in a way that implies they are ZmaxLab clients.

## Review method
Every asset was individually inspected (video assets via extracted frame). Checked for: visible third-party business branding, identifiable minors, topical fit with ZmaxLab's actual audience (adult-focused solo/small practices — NP, PA, mental health, dental, chiro, PT), geographic plausibility for a US-market business, and general quality.

## Required before any of this ships
- **Confirm Pexels license terms** cover commercial use in the intended context. Pexels' standard license generally permits commercial use, but does not permit implying endorsement by depicted people — which reinforces the rule below.
- **Never caption stock clinicians as clients or testimonial subjects.** The testimonial section's existing "illustrative" disclaimer must not be undermined by pairing quotes with stock faces, which would read as fabricated proof.
- **Optimise before shipping.** Several files are 3–8 MB and up to 8192px; the 4K video is 17 MB. These must be resized/compressed (and video converted to a web-friendly poster + compressed MP4/WebM) or they will severely damage the page-speed advantage gained earlier in this project.

## Honest note
Of 20 assets reviewed in detail, 7 were rejected outright and 3 need cropping or care — a ~50% usable rate. The two most valuable finds (the blue-background NP portrait and the two-clinician 4K video) are genuinely strong and worth building sections around.

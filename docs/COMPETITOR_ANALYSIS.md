## Adjacent / aspirational competitors (established healthcare marketing agencies)

### Intrepy Healthcare Marketing (intrepy.com)
- White background, one accent color (coral/orange), generous whitespace
- Shows real client work in laptop/phone device-frame mockups instead of fabricated stats
- Real photography of clinics and patients (e.g. an orthopedic clinic, a sunrise runner)

### NKP Medical (nkpmedical.com)
- Navy + warm gold palette, full-bleed real photography, row of real client logos (dermatology, oral surgery, weight loss center) as social proof
- "20 years, 100% medical marketing" positioning — experience as trust signal

## Pattern across all of them
- Light/white backgrounds, not dark mode
- One or two restrained accent colors, not a multi-color gradient rainbow
- Real photography or real product screenshots — never fabricated dashboards/analytics
- Credibility via client logos, real portfolio pieces, or years-in-business — not invented percentage claims

- Founder-built, not agency — a real differentiator if kept honest and specific (Ravi personally builds every site)
